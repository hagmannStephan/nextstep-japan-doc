# Visa Extension

Your initial visa is only valid for 90 days.
From **30 days before expiration**, you can extend your tourist visa.

::: tip
Extending your visa can take **up to 4 hours**. Try to visit early in the day to beat the crowds.
:::

## Preparation

1. Look up your local immigration office as well as the opening hours
    - [Osaka Immigration Office](https://maps.app.goo.gl/qkPzkVpGPcX1JTsT9)
        - Opens at 9:00, closes at 16:00
        - Enter on the side of the building marked "Immigration" 

2. Prepare the items you'll need at the immigration office
    - Passport
    - 10'000 ¥ cash (subject to change)
    - **Optional but recommended:** Booked flight. They ask if you already booked a flight back so that you can prove you don't want to overstay your visa. This makes a good impression and can save you some money.

## Extending the Visa

The process at the immigration office is different for every office and every visit. The events recalled below are from the Osaka Immigration Office and should just provide you with rough guidance.

### A - Enter the Building
1. You'll see a waiting area. Walk past it and take the stairs to your left to the next floor.
2. There will probably already be people waiting in line. Walk past them into the big room to your left.

### B - Information Counter (Possible to do on a separate day)
3. There are colored lines on the ground. Follow the yellow one to the information desk. You will probably have to wait a bit on the numbered seats until you're up (Seats 1–6; you will start at the lowest available seat and move down by one until you reach seat Nr. 1. Then you will be up next.).
4. When it's your turn, show them your passport and tell them you want to extend your tourist visa.
5. You will be handed some forms. Fill them out as truthfully as possible (you can write that you are participating in an unpaid internship or tourism and you want to extend your visa). Some values on the form and recommended answers are:
    - **Type of Visa**: Temporary Visitor
    - **Reason for extension**: Tourism
    - **Personal wealth**: Your wealth across all your bank accounts in Yen (approx.)
    - **Places you stayed at / want to visit**: Just the city names should be good
    - **Small Text**: You'll also have to write a small text about why you want to extend your visa. You are expected to write 3–5 sentences, where you can write about your internship, the Japanese culture, e.g.

### C - Buy Stamps

**Do not buy the stamp before they tell you to.**

6. Once you complete step B, you will be sent off to another information window, where you'll probably be directed to yet another information window. You will get informed about how much the stamp for extension costs and where you can buy them (you pay indirectly with stamps, not money). **They will keep your passport, so from there on you will have to finish the process on this day.**
7. Optional: If you don't have enough cash with you, you can go to the nearest ATM.
8. You will have to exit the room where you are currently in and go to the small stamp shop on the same floor right across (it looks a bit like a kombini). You can just show them the receipt you've been handed and give them the cash (no card).
9. If you still have a number from before, go wait at the counter where they took your passport (probably counter 3 or 4). Else wait at the long line for reception (green line) or go to the information counter (yellow line).

### D - Get your passport back
10. Maybe you will get called up and handed a new number or your passport directly. This may take quite a while. You will probably receive your passport from counter Nr. 3. Once you get your passport back with the extension stamp, check it. Afterwards, you can leave.
